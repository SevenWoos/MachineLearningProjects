### Comparison to Paper (Harutyunyan & Kumar, 2026)

Our TF-IDF + LR results closely reproduce the paper's numbers,
which serves as agood sanity check that the pipeline is implemented
correctly (same official train/dev/test split, same model config).

| Metric           | Paper (LR) | Ours   |
|------------------|-----------:|-------:|
| Subset Accuracy  | ~0.24      | 0.264  |
| Hamming Loss     | ~0.054     | 0.053  |
| Micro F1         | ~0.51      | 0.510  |
| Macro F1         | ~0.45      | 0.456  |

* Essentially a match — small differences likely come from our
  exact TF-IDF config (ngram_range=(1,2), min_df=2), which the
  paper didn't fully specify.

* Confirms our multi-hot matrix + Binary Relevance setup is
  correct BEFORE moving on to more complex models (BERT), where
  bugs would be harder to catch without this baseline to compare
  against.

* Next: paper's BERT run got Macro F1 = 0.49 (vs our 0.456 LR),
  beating both LR and BiLSTM on Macro F1 and Subset Accuracy —
  expect a similar jump once we fine-tune BERT with a multi-label
  head on this task.
